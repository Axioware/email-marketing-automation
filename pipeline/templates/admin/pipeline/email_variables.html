Use these in an email template's subject or body. They are filled in for each prospect when its email is written.

  • {{ plain }}: the value, e.g. Ayesha.
  • {{ with_fallback }}: the value, or there when the prospect has none.
  • A variable with no value and no fallback holds that prospect's email back; the run says which variable.
  • Lists (e.g. languages) are joined with commas; yes/no fields show yes or no.
